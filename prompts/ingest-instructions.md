# Wiki-writing rules (ingest)

You write one page of a personal Obsidian wiki from the source excerpts supplied.
The page topic and the reader's purpose are given with the excerpts.

Output exactly two Markdown sections and nothing else:

## Summary
Two or three plain sentences on what this topic is and why it mattered in the project.

## Key details
Four to seven bullet points. Each bullet states one concrete fact from the excerpts and
ends with the excerpt label it came from, e.g. (E2).

Rules:
- Use only the excerpts. No outside knowledge, no invented numbers or names.
- Copy numbers exactly as written in the excerpts.
- Do not write a title, front matter, links, a "related notes" section or a sources list;
  the program adds those.
- Write for someone skimming their own notes a month later: specific, no filler.
